# Executive Summary

## Purpose

This project analyzes 200,000 synthetic hospital encounters across a two-year period to understand patient flow and hospital operations.

## Headline Results

- Total encounters: 200,000
- Total patients: 78,000
- Admission rate: 19.93%
- Average wait: 60.4 minutes
- Average LOS: 4.43 days
- Bed occupancy: 54.91%
- 30-day readmission rate: 2.017%

## Operational Signals

Orthopedics had the highest encounter volume, Neurology had the highest average waiting time, and General Medicine had the highest average LOS. Doctor approval was the largest contributor to total discharge-delay hours in the generated data.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how SQL and Python can turn hospital operational data into practical management metrics. The Power BI-ready model packages these measures into a simple structure suitable for interactive reporting.

The dataset is synthetic and intended for analytics/research demonstration only.

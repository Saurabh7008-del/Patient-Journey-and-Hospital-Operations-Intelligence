# Operational Recommendations

Recommendations are based on the observed operational patterns.

1. Review staffing and patient-flow allocation in Neurology during high-demand periods because it has the highest average waiting time.
2. Review inpatient workflow in General Medicine because it has the highest average LOS.
3. Track doctor-approval turnaround as a discharge-management KPI because it contributes the largest total delay hours.
4. Monitor Neurology bed utilization closely and review admission/discharge coordination during high-occupancy periods.
5. Review appointment reminders and scheduling practices in Pediatrics to reduce no-shows.
6. Use department-level volume, waiting time and occupancy together rather than relying on a single KPI.
7. Add a weekly operational review of the 30 business-question metrics to identify changes early.
8. Use the dashboard as an operational monitoring tool, not as a clinical decision-support system.

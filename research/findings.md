# Key Findings

The findings below are calculated from the validated synthetic dataset of 200,000 encounters.

1. **Patient volume:** Orthopedics handled the highest encounter volume with 20,169 encounters.
2. **Waiting time:** Neurology recorded the highest average waiting time at 60.8 minutes.
3. **Length of Stay:** General Medicine had the highest average inpatient stay at 4.48 days.
4. **Discharge delays:** Doctor approval contributed the largest total delay time at 25,538.9 hours.
5. **Bed utilization:** Neurology recorded the highest occupancy rate at 56.26%.
6. **Appointments:** Pediatrics had the highest department-level appointment no-show rate at 10.37%.
7. **Overall admission rate:** 19.93% of encounters resulted in admission.
8. **Overall waiting time:** Average hospital waiting time was 60.4 minutes.
9. **Overall Length of Stay:** Average inpatient LOS was 4.43 days.
10. **Readmission:** The calculated 30-day readmission rate was 2.017%.

These are operational observations from synthetic data and are not clinical conclusions.
